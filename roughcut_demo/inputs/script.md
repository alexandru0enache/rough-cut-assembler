**Dragon Ball**

To start us off, Goku would be the strongest character in the series.

For a solid 3 chapters.

Goku was already a mini-powerhouse at the start of Dragon Ball.

The kid with a monkey tail could crush massive boulders with his bare fists and take a bullet point-blank without breaking a sweat.

The one to dethrone him and start an insane 100+ chapter run on top was Master Roshi.

Shortly after meeting Bulma, Goku met Master Roshi, the legendary martial artist who also taught his late grandfather.

Back then, Roshi had Goku beat by every possible metric.

That was Mercenary Tao Pai Pai, the first villain to come close to putting Goku in an early grave.

It took a trip to Korrin's Tower for Goku to finally outmatch the assassin, and even then Roshi was still the strongest.
